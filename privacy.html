Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Summary

This website does not use cookies, analytics, ads or trackers, and it does not collect any personal information from visitors. MiniToons never collects personal information from children.

MiniToons Publisher and TikTok

MiniToons Publisher is our internal tool for posting our own videos to our own TikTok account. When the MiniToons account owner signs in with TikTok, TikTok gives the tool:

  • basic account information (display name and account ID), to confirm the right account is connected;
  • an access token, used only to upload and publish MiniToons videos to that account.

The token is stored privately on our own computer, is never shared or sold, and is used for nothing else. We do not access, collect or store information about TikTok viewers, followers or any other users. The account owner can disconnect the tool at any time in TikTok's settings (Security → Manage app permissions), which revokes the token.

YouTube

We publish to our own YouTube channel with Google's YouTube Data API. The same rules apply: only our own account, no viewer data collected.

Children

Our videos are made for children, but this website and our tools collect nothing from anyone. On YouTube our videos are marked "made for kids", so comments and personalised ads are turned off.
