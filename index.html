MiniToons Publisher

MiniToons Publisher is the internal tool we use to share our own finished videos to the MiniToons accounts on YouTube and TikTok. It is used only by the MiniToons team for our own accounts. It is not offered to the public and does not collect information from viewers.

How it works: a video is made and reviewed by a person first. Only after that person approves it does the Publisher upload it, with its caption and hashtags, to the MiniToons TikTok account using TikTok's official Content Posting API.
